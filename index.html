Reflection

This portfolio gathers the projects I built during my first year of the Noroff Frontend Development program. Each one marks a step in my learning journey — from writing my first semantic HTML and styling with CSS, to thinking about responsive layouts, accessibility, and clean, reusable code.

Looking back, the biggest growth has been in how I plan a project before writing code: breaking a design into components, naming things consistently, and testing across screen sizes. My next goals are to deepen my JavaScript skills and to improve the accessibility and performance of everything I ship.
